# Experiment semantics

An **experiment** is a named attempt to investigate a technical or artistic question with identifiable inputs, method, output and observed result.

Its record should distinguish:

- **Question / hypothesis:** What was being investigated?
- **Method / variables:** How can the attempt be repeated or inspected?
- **Inputs / dependencies:** Which public assets, code and settings were used?
- **Observations / evidence:** What was actually observed, including failures and uncertainty?
- **Outcome:** What succeeded, failed, changed or remains unknown?
- **Limitations / next work:** What is unverified or provisional?

A failed experiment may have more research value than a polished prototype. No experiment needs artistic approval to remain in the public repository.

Technical status and gallery approval are **orthogonal**: a valid, reproducible failure may remain unshowcased; an owner-selected experiment may appear in the gallery without falsely claiming its technical hypothesis succeeded.

Never use private conversations as experiment evidence in this public project. Describe relevant technical observations neutrally and independently.
