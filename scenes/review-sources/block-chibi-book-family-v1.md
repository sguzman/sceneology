# Block-Chibi Book Family v1

**Status:** passed / user-approved

## Specimens

- Upright Shelf Book — slim blue book with visible spine.
- Closed Desk Book — thicker pink journal-like book lying flat.
- Open Reading Book — gold-covered book with two shallow page halves around a center hinge.

## Camera policy

Default beauty review uses perspective 3/4. Orthographic axis views remain available for geometry QA.

## Review gate

1. Do all three immediately read as books rather than generic blocks?
2. Does the upright one look appropriate for a shelf?
3. Does the flat one read as a closed desk/journal book?
4. Does the open one read as one connected open book?
5. Are cover/page layers stable while orbiting?
6. Do all three belong to the same cute block-chibi room style?


## Human review — pass

The book family was accepted.

Observed:

- all three read immediately as books;
- the upright specimen reads naturally as a shelf book;
- the flat specimen reads as a closed desk / journal book;
- the open specimen clearly reads as one open book;
- state variation is more important here than size variation, and that variation is successful.

A separate review-control bug was reported: perspective mouse-wheel zoom direction was inverted.
That control bug is tracked independently and does not change the book-family verdict.
