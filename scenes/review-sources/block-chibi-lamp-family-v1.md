# Block-Chibi Lamp Family v1

**Status:** failed / retained specimen

## Specimens

- Cozy Table Lamp — compact pink bedside/desk lamp.
- Tall Floor Lamp — room-corner floor lamp with cream shade.
- Block Task Lamp — chunky adjustable-work-lamp silhouette with connected arm/joint/head chain.

## Camera and input policy

Default beauty review uses perspective 3/4.

Mouse wheel convention is now standardized:

- wheel up = zoom in;
- wheel down = zoom out.

Orthographic axis views remain geometry QA only.

## Review gate

1. Do all three immediately read as lamps?
2. Is the warm emitter region understandable?
3. Do table/floor/task support strategies read distinctly?
4. Does the task lamp read as one connected adjustable fixture rather than an abstract crane?
5. Are all parts stable under camera motion?
6. Does the family remain cute and block-chibi?


## Human review — failed

The family is not accepted as v1.

Observed:

- two specimens are geometrically coherent enough;
- the task lamp reads as broken: its support/head chain appears disconnected and the top/head does not read correctly;
- the floor-lamp pink-base / cream-shade split is not geometrically wrong, but the palette feels awkward;
- several slanted surfaces look dirty/stained, likely from review-light shadow artifacts rather than intended material;
- the visible light/glow is much too weak.

Correction target for v1.1:

1. rebuild task lamp as a visibly continuous support chain;
2. clean shadow rendering with explicit bias/normal-bias and better shadow-map quality;
3. strengthen emissive and local lamp lights substantially;
4. unify the fixture palette while retaining warm emitters.

The failed v1 assets remain preserved.
